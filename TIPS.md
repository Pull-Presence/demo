# Getting the most out of Pull Presence

- **The pill follows you around the pull request.** Conversation, Commits and Files changed all count as being here.
- **Idle means away.** If someone hasn't touched the page for a minute, they show as idle instead of active.
- **Want to review quietly?** Open the Pull Presence popup and switch to Invisible. You'll still see everyone else.
- **It only shows people with Pull Presence.** Share https://pullpresence.com with your team so you can see each other.
